Stride Themes

Complete designs for a Stride site: design tokens, a component library, page templates, photographs and fonts. Applying a theme never changes or deletes your existing pages, and Undo brings your previous design tokens back.

To install one, open Admin → Themes in Stride, pick a theme and choose Install. Preview shows your own pages in the theme before anything is saved; Apply makes it live. Stride downloads the theme itself and checks its signature before installing it.

Atelier

Editorial theme for studios and agencies: serif type, paper, ink, terracotta.

  • agency
  • portfolio
  • business

Atelier is a theme for creative studios, agencies and independent practices that want their site to read like a well-made book. It pairs a high-contrast editorial serif (Instrument Serif) with a precise grotesque (Inter Tight), sets everything on warm off-white paper with ink-black type, and reserves a single terracotta accent for the moments that matter: a rule, a numeral, a testimonial band.

Grove

Harbor

A warm, appetizing theme for restaurants, cafes and local food businesses.

  • restaurant
  • business

Harbor is built for places people eat, drink and linger: neighbourhood restaurants, cafes, bakeries, wine bars and small local businesses. Warm linen and toasted-sand surfaces sit under a deep harbour teal and a terracotta accent, with an expressive Fraunces serif for headlines and friendly, rounded Nunito for everything else.

Ledger

A composed navy-and-slate theme for consultancies, law firms and finance.

  • business
  • agency

Ledger is built for firms whose clients are trusting them with something that matters: law firms, consultancies, accountants, wealth managers and advisory boutiques. Midnight navy and cool slate carry the page, a restrained brass accent marks rules and labels, and a crisp Inter Tight / Inter pairing with tabular figures keeps every number honest.

Nightfall

Dark, image-led photography portfolio: full-bleed plates, quiet serif, gold.

  • portfolio
  • personal

Nightfall is a portfolio theme for photographers and visual artists who want the work to do the talking. Pages sit on a warm darkroom black so photographs glow the way they do on a lightbox, and a single safelight-gold accent marks the few things that matter: an index number, a button, one italic line in the footer.

Pulse

Dark-first SaaS landing theme: violet glow, bold geometric type, pricing, FAQ.

Pulse is a launch-ready theme for software products and startups. It is dark first: a near-black page with a violet cast, lit by soft radial glows in the brand violet (#6D28D9) and a magenta partner, with headlines set tight in Sora and running text in Inter.
